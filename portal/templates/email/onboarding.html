3. Set availability after signing in

Open Availability in your parent account and choose a child under Availability for student. Add the times they can attend and choose Save weekly hours. Mark preferred times if helpful. Use Temporary availability for holidays, extra hours or time away, then Save temporary hours. Repeat for each child; their calendars are separate. Check the timezone shown on the page.

4. Find and open a lesson

Open Lessons to see booked lessons. Choose the child and lesson, then Details to view the time, tutor, lesson plan and discussion. If no lessons appear, check with your tutor.

5. Use the shared whiteboard

When the scheduled lesson start time arrives, open its details and choose Open whiteboard. Parents and students can enter from that time onward. Use Pen to draw, Text to type and Select to move items. Scroll to move around and use the zoom controls or Fit all to adjust the view. You and the tutor see changes live. Add a PDF or image with PDF / image, and use Download PNG to save a snapshot.

6. Join the lesson call

In the whiteboard, choose Join call. Allow your browser to use the camera and microphone, check the preview and microphone level, choose your devices, then select Join lesson call. Use the camera and microphone buttons to switch them on or off, open call settings to change devices, and choose Leave when finished. Headphones help prevent echo.

7. Reschedule a lesson

From your parent account, open an upcoming lesson and choose Request change. Select an available time in the calendar, add an optional reason and choose Send request. Your tutor must approve the change; the original booking stays in place until it is approved. You can follow the request from Lessons. Requests made less than 24 hours before the lesson require acknowledgement that the original lesson may be charged.

8. Cancel a lesson

Open an upcoming lesson in your parent account and choose Cancel lesson. You can try rescheduling first. Add an optional reason, confirm your decision and choose Confirm cancellation. Cancellation takes effect immediately. A late cancellation also requires you to acknowledge the cancellation terms.

Cancellations made less than 24 hours before the scheduled start may be charged at the full lesson rate, at the tutor’s discretion under your contract. Cancellations made at least 24 hours before the start are free.

9. Follow your children’s progress

In Lessons, choose Past lessons & feedback and open a lesson’s feedback to read the tutor’s parent update. Visit Course plans for learning plans and goals, and Homework for assignments, completion, grades and tutor feedback as they become available.

10. Access invoices

Open Invoices in your parent account to check amounts, due dates and payment status. Choose Download beside an invoice to save its PDF. Invoice emails also include a download link; sign in to your parent account when prompted.
