3. SET AVAILABILITY AFTER SIGNING IN
Open Availability in your parent account:
{{ links.availability }}
Choose a child under "Availability for student", add times they can attend and choose "Save weekly hours". Mark preferred times if helpful. Use "Temporary availability" for holidays, extra hours or time away, then "Save temporary hours". Repeat for each child; their calendars are separate. Check the timezone shown on the page.

4. FIND AND OPEN A LESSON
Open Lessons:
{{ links.lessons }}
Choose the child and booked lesson, then "Details" to view the time, tutor, lesson plan and discussion. If no lessons appear, check with your tutor.

5. USE THE SHARED WHITEBOARD
When the scheduled lesson start time arrives, open the lesson details and choose "Open whiteboard". Parents and students can enter from that time onward. Use "Pen" to draw, "Text" to type and "Select" to move items. Scroll to move around and use the zoom controls or "Fit all" to adjust the view. Changes are shared live with the tutor. Add a PDF or image with "PDF / image", and use "Download PNG" to save a snapshot.

6. JOIN THE LESSON CALL
In the whiteboard, choose "Join call". Allow your browser to use the camera and microphone, check the preview and microphone level, choose your devices, then "Join lesson call". Use the camera and microphone buttons to switch them on or off, open call settings to change devices, and choose "Leave" when finished. Headphones help prevent echo.

7. RESCHEDULE A LESSON
From your parent account, open an upcoming lesson and choose "Request change". Select an available time, add an optional reason and choose "Send request". Your tutor must approve the change; the original booking stays in place until approved. Follow the request from Lessons. Requests made less than 24 hours before the lesson require acknowledgement that the original lesson may be charged.

8. CANCEL A LESSON
Open an upcoming lesson in your parent account and choose "Cancel lesson". You can try rescheduling first. Add an optional reason, confirm your decision and choose "Confirm cancellation". Cancellation takes effect immediately. A late cancellation also requires acknowledgement of the cancellation terms.

CANCELLATION FEE: Cancellations made less than 24 hours before the scheduled start may be charged at the full lesson rate, at the tutor's discretion under your contract. Cancellations made at least 24 hours before the start are free.
